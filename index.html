Morningo

An alarm clock with wake-up missions, morning plans and tips for iPhone.

Support

Need help? Write to hoki.app.support@gmail.com — we usually reply within 1–2 days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

The alarm didn't ring.
Open Settings → Morningo and make sure Alarms and Notifications are allowed. Check that the alarm is switched on in the app. Keep the app installed and your iPhone charged.

A mission doesn't work.
Steps needs Motion & Fitness, Photo spot and Barcode need the Camera, Say your plan needs the Microphone. You can allow them in Settings → Morningo. If you can't do a mission right now, tap "Can't do it right now?" to switch to a simple math task.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Morningo → Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the app → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I request a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
