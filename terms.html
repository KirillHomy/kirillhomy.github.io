Alarms — important

The app relies on iOS to ring alarms. We do our best to make alarms reliable, but we can't guarantee that an alarm will ring in every case (for example if the device is off, out of battery, muted by system settings, or iOS limits the app). Don't use Morningo as your only alarm for anything critical, such as medication, flights or safety.

Missions and tips

Missions like Steps or Photo spot ask you to move around. Use them only where it's safe. Morning tips and coach messages are general wellbeing suggestions, not medical advice.
