Privacy Policy — Morningo

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Morningo ("the app") is made by Kirill Khomicevich ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. We built Morningo so that almost everything stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

These are stored only on your iPhone and never sent to us:

  • Alarms, missions, goals, morning plans, streaks and check-ins. Saved in the app's local storage.
  • Say your plan (microphone). Speech is recognized on your iPhone while you read your goal out loud. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photo spot and Barcode (camera). The reference photo you save stays on your device. Matching photos and reading barcodes happen on your iPhone.
  • Steps (motion). The step count is read from your iPhone's motion sensor only during the mission.
  • Coach tips. Written by Apple's on-device model. Your plan and answers are not sent to us or to any server.
  • Streak card. Saved to your Photos only when you tap Save; the app can only add photos, not read them.

What leaves your device

Weather (location). If you allow location access, your approximate location is sent to Apple WeatherKit to look up the forecast. It is not stored by us. You can turn location off at any time in Settings.

Purchases (Adapty). Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use Adapty to check whether you have Pro and to show the right offer. Adapty receives an anonymous app user ID, your device identifier for vendors (IDFV), your purchase history and, if you allow tracking, your advertising identifier (IDFA), and Apple Search Ads attribution data. Adapty's privacy policy: adapty.io/privacy

Analytics (Amplitude). We collect anonymous usage events, such as which screens are opened, which missions are completed and app sessions, to understand what helps people wake up and to fix problems. Events are linked to a random device ID, not to your name or email. Amplitude's privacy policy: amplitude.com/privacy

Tracking. The app asks for permission to use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to measure where installs come from. It is used only if you tap Allow, and you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

What we don't collect

No name, email, phone number, contacts, health data, voice recordings or precise location history. You don't need an account to use the app.

Notifications

Alarms and reminders are scheduled on your iPhone with Apple's AlarmKit and local notifications. We do not send push messages from a server.

Content Hub and TikTok

Content Hub is our internal desktop tool that publishes our own videos to our own TikTok accounts. When we connect one of our accounts through TikTok Login Kit, Content Hub receives that account's basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) and an access token. The token is stored only in the macOS Keychain on our own computer; it is never sent to any server other than TikTok's. Content Hub uses the token only to upload and publish videos we approve, and does not access any other TikTok users' data. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is deleted when you delete the app. Anonymous analytics and purchase records kept by Amplitude and Adapty are held for as long as needed for the purposes above. To ask us to delete data linked to your device ID, write to the email below.

Children

Morningo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Write to us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
